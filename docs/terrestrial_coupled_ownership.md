# Independent terrestrial water and energy ownership

The internal `TerrestrialCoupledOwner` advances exposed-land W, surface enthalpy H, and atmospheric energy Ea from one accepted restart to one common time. It is an explicit successor alongside the [prescribed W/H owner](terrestrial_water_ownership.md). Ordinary generation does not initialize or advance it; the annual ice and precipitation models retain their existing authority.

## Physical and source scope

Each column has fixed area, dry heat capacity, atmospheric capacity, gray-layer absorptivity, sensible exchange coefficient, and shortwave albedo. Water properties share one freezing reference. The surface temperature follows the existing solid / mixed / liquid enthalpy inversion. Atmospheric energy is `Ca*(Ta−Tf)`; an airless column requires zero Ea, absorptivity and sensible exchange. Lakes and marine cells have explicit inactive zero slots. Columns exchange no lateral water, heat or air.

One macro interval lies wholly inside one constant incident-shortwave span. Its optional initial source batch contains prescribed solid/liquid imports and initial-liquid withdrawals. For a nonempty batch the instantaneous mass operator runs once; an empty batch carries the state directly. Mass events preserve Ea bitwise and cannot fund withdrawals with simultaneous imports or later melting. The actual rounded carried joules feed the liquid outbox. Reusing a consumed event ID refuses. A forcing ID binds an exact window and vector; changed or conflicting source histories refuse.

The manual overload accepts a finite sequence of phase-event, within-branch or fixed-duration plans and proposed tubes for each exposed cell. The owner supplies the actual current state, properties, area, forcing and clock to each thermal call. Per-segment plans cannot override the carried restart, and the owner cannot promote an offline certificate over a refused native result. A phase event before the common endpoint requires a continuation; unused suffixes and incomplete coverage refuse, except for an explicitly designated zero-remainder terminal skip on a final within-branch entry.

The [automatic overload](terrestrial_automatic_continuation.md) accepts explicit initial imports/withdrawals followed by fixed-W thermal continuation under one immutable forcing span. It derives branches, tubes and durations privately, retaining all rejected trials and counting their work. Its per-step allowance is a fixed share of the headroom after the certified initial source jump, and raw carry also checks the actual outward cumulative error increase. All cells still require the same complete endpoint before the existing opaque candidate can commit. It reuses the manual path’s initial mass-event and jump certificates exactly once for a nonempty batch, skips A for an empty batch, and does not replace the production climate model.

## Clock, errors and publication

Every segment uses the same power-of-two clock quantum derived from the common endpoint. Starts, durations, caps and endpoints must lie exactly on that lattice within its binary64 integer range. Selected endpoints are never rounded after solving. The original phase-event and within-branch goals retain their strict branch/domain gates; an unresolved latent-boundary handoff still refuses under those goals without shifting H or changing a tolerance.

The explicit [fixed-duration continuation](terrestrial_fixed_duration_continuation.md) can carry the raw state across a phase boundary using the same global thermal equations. Its initial branch annotation must remain true, but its numerical stages and endpoint may change phase. A proved finite-horizon physical tube supplies a same-time endpoint enclosure and energy-error bound; no first-hit or no-hit claim is made. The owner requires the exact planned endpoint and carries the local bound into the inherited error history. This provides a continuation for the represented-upper-boundary case without rewriting the state or weakening the old phase goals.

The owner also accepts the opt-in [native Hermite residual certificate](terrestrial_native_residual_certificate.md) for fixed-duration plans. It reserves the complete declared dyadic leaf work before physical calls, retains the direct tube separately and carries the selected joint residual bound forward without resetting inherited error. The first six-hour dry control certifies 103.695 J/m², while the six-hour melt control still refuses its unchanged 3340 J/m² target. This capability has one retained qualification-target failure; it is not an automatic scheduler or a production seasonal accuracy result.

The error scope is explicitly `canonical_projected_W_prescribed_import_J_reference_v1`. At each source event the reference uses the represented post-event W and the recorded canonical import joules. Original-source mass and energy trajectory accuracy is **not certified**. The receipt retains the original source operands and mass-projection and energy-publication discrepancies. This scope is narrower than physical accuracy relative to unrounded sources.

Within that scope, each column carries a same-time joint bound `E ≥ |H−Href|+|Ea−Earef|`. A local solve never resets E. At fixed W the physical positive-temperature flow is nonexpansive in this norm. For an initial liquid withdrawal of density q, the exact reference map is

`G(H) = H + I − q [Lf + cl max(H−W Lf,0)/(Cb+W cl)]`.

Gross withdrawals must be feasible for the whole inherited uncertainty interval. On that interval G is monotone and 1-Lipschitz, so the outgoing error is bounded by `E + |Hpublished−G(Hnumeric)|`, evaluated outward. Complete drainage retains dry-substrate heat. Export-energy uncertainty is retained separately and is not counted twice as state error. A zero withdrawal imposes no liquid requirement.

Each accepted thermal segment adds its certified local error. For a phase event, the event-state bound is transported from the true hit time to the selected numerical time using the proved tube bound on `|Fs|+|Fa|`. Full pre/post uncertainty boxes must remain above the exact surface and air energy floors, and the cumulative error must fit the immutable owner policy. Conservative interval arithmetic can refuse exact inventory equality; no tolerance is added to admit it. Local first-hit labels describe the canonical point-start problem, not every state in the inherited uncertainty set.

Preparation keeps states, error bounds, source IDs, every generated thermal request/receipt, outbox and hydrology projection private until all cells cover the common endpoint. An opaque candidate binds one owner and its exact accepted base. Commit swaps a fully allocated bundle; stale, foreign and replayed candidates refuse. The separate work meter records spent preparation and solver work even when physical state is rejected. The generated surface must still match its captured epoch, and the pipeline forbids a second owner mode from claiming that epoch.

The outbox's hydrology projection uses the existing supplied-liquid partition once over the common duration. It is not an external delivery acknowledgement, an evaporation energy budget or a replacement for annual precipitation consumers. Component receipts retain separate H/Ea storage and energy exchanges. The aggregate displayed ledger uses labeled ordered binary64 sums and retains its residual; small closure is not a trajectory-accuracy certificate.

## Bounded use and evidence

The manual-plan path accepts explicit per-segment and per-preparation limits, capped by the implementation. It reserves the complete planned work before mass or thermal calls and does not retry, widen a tube, increase a tolerance, insert balancing heat or adapt a source schedule after refusal. The automatic path has its separately documented bounded proposal and retry policy. Receipt/work limits bound the retained numerical structure, not process RSS or arbitrary caller-retained candidate copies. Allocation failure can prevent diagnostic publication while leaving the accepted physical bundle unchanged.
