# Production cryosphere: surface domains, conserved state and integration

This research identified two separate production defects. The annual temperature gate still makes ablation unreachable wherever the model creates ice. The historical exposed-land definition also admitted lake cells into the grounded-glacier calculation; that domain defect is now corrected. Seasonal accumulation and melt still require a stateful water-and-energy model, a different thermal interface, and a new handoff to hydrology.

The grounded-ice domain and sediment successor preserve historical replay. Native reservoir ownership and the phase-aware surface/air component are now joined by an explicit internal independent-column W/H/Ea owner. Production seasonal scheduling, original-source accuracy and downstream replacement remain open. This document retains the research and engineering requirements; the linked execution evidence establishes each component's narrower scope.

Implementation update: the [grounded-domain correction is now adopted](../runs/grounded-ice-domain-adoption/README.md), including native eligibility, current/historical validation, transport-source linkage and public displays. The archived research snapshot predates that adoption. Seasonal accumulation, reachable ablation and conserved mass/energy evolution remain open; the domain correction does not establish those capabilities.

## 1. Current physical meaning and confirmed defects

`derive_cryosphere_state` in [environment.cpp](../cpp/src/engine/environment.cpp) initializes thickness from annual temperature, annual precipitation, latitude and elevation. It resets its outputs on every call. It does not accumulate snow through successive years, debit an ocean or atmospheric water inventory, or advance a glacier volume. The later velocity, erosion and surface mass-balance fields are diagnostic proxies associated with that constructed thickness.

Positive thickness requires a positive cold factor `(-Tannual - 3) / 22`, so every created icy cell has annual temperature below −3 °C. The ablation expression is proportional to `max(0, Tannual + 1.5)` and is consequently zero on every such cell. The separate warm-thickness reduction above −2 °C is unreachable for the same reason. These are algebraic properties of the current source, independent of any calibration scenario. The [retained Earth diagnosis](../runs/earth-ice-calibration-review/README.md) also contains icy cells with positive summer monthly temperatures and no diagnosed ablation.

A glacier is a persistent, gravity-driven ice body originating on land; surviving seasonal accumulation is part of its formation. An annual temperature threshold alone does not demonstrate that winter snow survives summer, and an annual-rate diagnostic is not a history of glacier growth.[^1] This distinction is especially important here because the exported Python histories start from the terminal native diagnosis and apply additional empirical recurrences rather than evolving the native state.

The lake problem is independently observable. Native lake cells normally have `is_lake=true` and `is_water=false`, because the latter identifies marine water in this pipeline. The pre-correction ice constructor excluded only `is_water`; the adopted grounded-domain correction now excludes both wet flags. In the retained historical-climate fixture `tests/fixtures/cli_ecology_parent_availability/legacy.json.gz`, lake cells 4 and 7 have water depths of about 174.44 and 84.15 m, yet carry diagnostic ice thicknesses of 319.48 and 386.46 m. Cell 7 also points its glacier flow to cell 4 and carries 0.1528 m of glacial erosion potential. These are retained historical-world observations, not newly generated seasonal-world witnesses.[^7]

Lake freezing is a different surface process. Measurements over a seasonally ice-covered lake examine heat exchange across snow, ice, water and atmosphere; the change from open water to ice cover alters that exchange.[^2] Excluding a lake from this grounded-glacier proxy therefore must not be presented as evidence that the lake has no ice. The current model has no resolved freshwater lake-ice state from which to make that inference.

The native sheet builder introduces a second distinction: active thick-ice members and contextual sheet associations. Its breadth-first traversal uses thickness above 25 m, while a later pass associates some zero-ice cells with nearby sheets and assigns historical terrain proxies. The retained pre-correction cold world contains 49 ordinary marine cells with sheet associations despite zero thickness; the adopted correction removes those associations. An association is not proof of active grounded ice. Some glacial lakes and fjords can legitimately retain glacial context; ordinary deep ocean should not acquire it simply through the index-order propagation of nearby associations.[^8]

## 2. Execution order and water ownership

The relevant native order is fresh sea level and marine labeling → climate → water budget → flow and lakes, with numerical-depression retries as needed. After erosion, the pipeline derives ice, applies one bulk glacial sediment transfer, restabilizes sea level/climate/hydrology, and derives terminal ice again. The second derivation prevents stale terminal temperature from being used, but both derivations reset the ice diagnosis. Neither is a physical elapsed-time step. The [current native dependency review](../runs/production-cryosphere-transition-research/native-dependencies/FINDINGS.md) identifies the exact interfaces and source hashes.

The seasonal thermal solver uses a combined surface-plus-atmosphere heat capacity and one temperature per column. Its effective top-of-atmosphere reflection and emission coefficients are prescribed. Lakes, grounded ice, snow phase change and sea ice do not modify those coefficients. A certificate for the existing sensible-heat equation is therefore not evidence for a latent-heat or snow-albedo feedback.

Precipitation is produced empirically after the periodic thermal solution. Hydrology then partitions annual precipitation into evapotranspiration, infiltration and runoff. Introducing a snow store after that allocation would retain precipitation that has already been allocated to other destinations. Adding melt to runoff without changing the original supply would create a second delivery of the same water. Conversely, simply subtracting snow without an explicit meltwater return would lose supply from downstream recharge, rivers and lakes.

The corrected interface must assign every supply once. For a terrestrial surface control volume, a useful accounting requirement is

`precipitation + lateral inflow − lateral outflow = evapotranspiration + infiltration + runoff + change in stored water`.

Stored water includes solid and liquid phases. This is an engineering conservation requirement, not a claim that all these processes are already resolved. If precipitation remains prescribed from outside the modeled reservoir, its import and carried enthalpy must be explicit. It cannot simultaneously be described as an internally closed atmospheric condensation cycle without a corresponding vapor and energy debit.

The GLASS snow model evolves liquid and solid water together with layer energy. It also treats liquid movement and phase change as coupled processes. Its experiments show that phase-change treatment can affect time-step dependence even when an implicit heat calculation is stable.[^3] This supports separate checks for water closure, energy closure and melt timing; it does not justify transferring that model's parameters or accuracy to this repository.

## 3. What the research supports—and what it does not

| Approach | Supported use | Missing evidence for this world generator |
|---|---|---|
| Annual cold/moisture threshold | Explicit static diagnostic with a declared domain | Seasonal survival, perennial mass, melt timing and water/energy budgets |
| Temperature-index melt | A calibrated relation between temperature and melt under a stated forcing climate | A portable physical response across this generator's orbital, atmospheric and stellar variations |
| Monthly energy-balance downscaling | A model with adequate radiation, precipitation and atmospheric forcing, plus declared submonthly assumptions | The required fields and independently justified downscaling are not all present here |
| Coupled freshwater enthalpy | Finite solid/liquid storage and heat accounting under a common reference | Production source scheduling, scalable spatial thermal coupling, full-period phase accuracy and authoritative downstream liquid delivery |
| Lake/sea-ice thermodynamics | Separate water-body freezing and surface-exchange models | Lake storage/heat profiles, saline phase behavior, ocean heat exchange and water partition |
| Grounded glacier dynamics | Evolution of ice mass, thickness and transport with boundary fluxes | A physical time axis, suitable initial state, firn/ice allocation and a mass-conserving dynamic solver |

The diurnal Energy Balance Model illustrates why monthly temperature and precipitation alone do not reconstruct a complete surface energy budget. Its forcing includes shortwave and longwave radiation, cloud cover and compatible surface elevation; it adds explicit temporal downscaling. Its paper also reports sensitivity of the temperature–melt relationship to orbital insolation changes and seasonal errors that can cancel in annual totals.[^4] Thus replacing the old ablation term with a positive-month average would address its dead branch but would not establish the intended general physical correction.

ZEMBA demonstrates a useful lower-complexity architecture: seasonal snow accumulation and energy-limited melt influence surface reflectivity, while a separate sea-ice component changes the ocean surface response. It is a zonally averaged energy-and-moisture model, not a parameter set validated for this irregular terrain mesh.[^5] Its relevance is the explicit coupling and return of excess melt energy to sensible heating, rather than permission to insert snow albedo directly into this code's existing top-of-atmosphere coefficient.

Enthalpy is an appropriate state variable because temperature alone does not encode latent energy when ice and water coexist. PISM uses enthalpy to conserve energy through the pressure-melting regime and to represent liquid-water energy within temperate ice.[^6] The repository's homogeneous freshwater calorimeter is a simpler system. Its fixed freezing-point, single-column phase relation must remain distinguished from pressure-dependent glacier thermodynamics and saline sea ice.

## 4. The immediate grounded-domain correction

The proposed native declaration is `exposed_land_annual_grounded_ice_diagnostic_v1`. Its surface eligibility is exactly `not is_water and not is_lake`. A typed `grounded_ice_surface_applicable` mirror makes that domain visible to validators and public consumers. Inapplicability gives structural zeros for the grounded diagnostic, not an estimate of absent lake or sea ice.

Active membership uses the roundtrip binary64 source field `grounded_ice_diagnostic_thickness_m`; the ordinary `ice_thickness_m` remains a display-precision value. A source thickness just above 25 m can round to exactly 25 m, so replaying membership from the displayed number would change the domain. Validation must account for that declared rounding while retaining the exact strict threshold. Standing freshwater and saline lakes can retain explicitly glacial-lake context, but neither becomes eligible for this grounded diagnostic.

The correction has five linked obligations:

1. Water-covered cells cannot originate active grounded thickness, surface mass balance, basal sliding, glacier velocity or erosion potential. Repeated derivation must reset stale values and the applicability mirror consistently.
2. Active sheet seeds and connectivity use eligible cells above the declared strict thickness threshold. A lake cannot connect two otherwise disconnected grounded components. Thin grounded donors and thick active sheet members remain distinct domains.
3. Contextual affiliation must be based on active neighboring ice rather than another already-associated margin. Explicit glacial lakes and fjords may retain context. That relation must not turn ordinary ocean cells into active members or propagate through a chain because of iteration order.
4. Glacial sediment may be delivered from an eligible donor into a lake or marine destination. Excluding wet donors must not discard valid wet recipients or alter the existing area/volume transfer identity.
5. Python consumers must reject contradictory current grounded inputs before deriving new products. Historical marine `ice_field` and other undeclared legacy behavior need their retained branch, rather than being silently reinterpreted under a new domain.

The sediment snapshot must bind eligibility at the time of transfer. The terminal world's lake flags cannot reconstruct that earlier state because restabilization can change water coverage. A transport-v3 declaration with a snapshot-v3 identity, exact grounded-source link and mandatory typed `is_lake` input gives an auditable source. Historical transport v2 retains its old equations and source schema. Deleting or mixing current declarations must fail instead of selecting a historical fallback.

Existing `ice_sheet_id` remains a possible context association. Stability diagnostics that intentionally use contextual margins must say so; they cannot be cited as active-member area or volume evidence. The existing `glaciated_land_fraction` denominator also remains unchanged: its historical nonmarine domain includes lakes. A future exposed-land fraction should receive a separate name and contract. The Earth-profile interval of 0.005–0.45 is not relaxed or repurposed by this repair.

## 5. Reuse the completed numerical work

The earlier studies already established useful components. The freshwater calorimeter owns finite mass and reference enthalpy, phase-limited initial withdrawals, conservative unequal-area transfers and atomic publication. The coupled surface/air prototype supplies separate thermal storage and cancelling internal exchanges. The higher-order stage and event-only carry experiments establish additional signed-stage, source-event and publication rules. These are implementation resources, not a complete production ice model.[^9]

The closed 256/512-interval study still misses its 0.01 kg/m² phase-mass goal, with sampled errors of approximately 0.03815 and 0.02228 kg/m². Its temperature and mean-radiation passes do not resolve phase crossings between sample points. The new [phase segment](../runs/seasonal-cryosphere-phase-segment-review/README.md) addresses the boundary-crossing mechanism through a fitted SDIRK2 duration and separate physical first-hit/error certificates. Its 22-request suite and retained spring-entry bound establish local component results; the closed whole-period study remains unresolved.

The existing mass primitive also distinguishes canonical movement energy from ideal source energy. Its retained extreme case has 1,025 J of ideal import energy but only 1 J in the rounded canonical movement sum. Small ledger residuals therefore do not establish original-source accuracy. Production receipts must retain source-representation error separately from arithmetic closure and thermal-discretization error.

The [native terrestrial reservoir object](terrestrial_water_ownership.md) now owns per-cell water mass and enthalpy, an explicit finalized epoch/restart, declared source movements, and one accepted state/outbox/hydrologic-projection bundle. Preparation retains source identities; a successful commit consumes each identity once. Depression correction and periodic trial iterates remain outside that finalized epoch. External one-use liquid delivery and replacement of the annual precipitation consumers are still pending. The old constructed glacier is not used as a validated initial reservoir.

The calorimeter now exposes a separate [instantaneous mass-event interface](../runs/seasonal-mass-event-integration-review/README.md). Imports and initial-inventory withdrawals produce a canonical post-event W/H without advancing time or supplying heat. That removes the positive-duration interface dependency before coupled thermal evolution. The event's state-rounding residual and original-source error still belong in the successor history; publishing a new restart does not erase them.

The [independent coupled owner](terrestrial_coupled_ownership.md) now supplies atmospheric restart ownership, one common endpoint and an opaque accepted receipt for explicit constant-forcing columns. Its same-time error history includes inherited energy uncertainty, event publication defects and thermal error under the declared canonical W/import-energy reference. It retains original source operands without claiming original-source trajectory accuracy. It does not supply conservative mesh exchanges or a production seasonal schedule. Astronomical forcing and mesh geometry can be reused, but existing effective emissivity does not uniquely determine the new gray-layer coefficients. Optical properties and the mapping between modeled atmospheric temperature and precipitation phase need explicit physical assumptions and independent verification. A successor monthly mass-and-energy certificate remains necessary before the new liquid supply or ice state becomes authoritative downstream.

## 6. Validation that can establish the change

The grounded-domain regression must demonstrate failure in the old implementation and success in the corrected one for the actual native lake flag convention. It should protect positive dry ice, exclude marine and freshwater active members, prevent a lake connectivity bridge, prevent context cascades, preserve explicit glacial-lake/fjord context and permit sediment delivery to water. A forged wet donor must fail independently of any healthy generator output.

The Python layer needs strict current/historical dispatch controls, malformed and missing mirrors, source-version disagreement, pre-transfer versus terminal lake-state distinctions, and atomic refusal before descendant mutation. Existing sediment finite-inventory and area-volume checks remain relevant. Merely comparing an updated producer with an updated copy of its equations would not independently establish the new domain.

At integration level, the complete native result must pass full, geography-only and summary publication with the same natural source identity. Current healthy and cold outputs need review for legitimate changed associations and unchanged physical coefficients. Original failing worlds and historical fixtures remain intact. Calibration is a separate question: a correct domain repair can change landforms, ecology or access through their existing dependencies without proving that glacier coverage resembles observations.

For the later stateful seasonal model, conservation checks must use initial and terminal reservoir states plus actual source/transfer integrals, with internal edges cancelling globally. Phase inventories must stay nonnegative and respect available donor mass. Source approximation, nonlinear solve error, publication rounding and temporal resolution need separate evidence. A steady repeated climate iteration is not a substitute for a declared physical simulation duration, and a one-year snow balance is not a perennial glacier forecast.
