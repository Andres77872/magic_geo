## One shared energy bound

Let Q=A·H be each retained store's extensive enthalpy and J each pending parcel's energy. Absorption adds selected J coordinates to their prescribed retained targets and removes those parcel coordinates. All other energy coordinates are unchanged. This nonnegative linear map has column sums one, so its induced L1 norm is one. The previously certified joint radius E therefore passes through once, even when several parcels share a target or originated in the same earlier event.

The implementation forms finite represented candidates for `W + sum(m)/A` and `H + sum(J)/A`. Long-double arithmetic is a candidate generator, not a claim of correctly rounded exact rational evaluation. Independent outward intervals enclose each exact expression and its difference from the represented output. The sum of area-weighted absolute new H projection defects is added to E. Earlier export projection errors and parcel uncertainty intervals are already covered by inherited E and are not charged again. Signed mass and energy projection residuals are retained separately; retained gain balances pending loss with no new external source.

Untouched W/H and surviving parcel payloads remain bit-identical. Target density, conductivity and nonwater capacity stay prescribed; the rebuilt graph accounts for changes in thickness, subsequent depths and conductance caused by receiving mass. Final admission checks the enlarged E ball for every active and positive deep store, including unchanged stores. The energy certificate remains conditional on canonical projected W and prescribed geometry; this does not propagate original-mass uncertainty through later thermodynamics or geometry.

## Ownership and dependency order

An owner request contains existing pending transaction/event identities and existing positive-water target addresses. It supplies no substitute mass, energy or temperature. The owner resolves those references against its accepted snapshot, rejects unknown or repeated parcels and invalid targets, and passes the complete retained records privately to the component. A standalone finite packet list is not an authenticated physical source. A newly constructed owner's seed remains an explicitly trusted canonical witness for a new lifetime.

Absorption is a separate zero-duration transaction, exclusive of material extraction, remapping and thermal forcing. Incoming material therefore cannot fund the initial inventory of a simultaneous withdrawal. Preparation leaves accepted state unchanged. Commit replaces recipient state, rebuilt graph, E, remaining parcels, receipt and revision together. The clock and consumed extraction IDs are unchanged. An identical committed request returns its retained result; a new request cannot receive a parcel already removed from the pending set.

This is an internal ownership transfer within one global owner. Explicit target selection prescribes the transport; it does not infer a hydrologic path, travel time, receiver capacity, drainage fraction or acknowledgment from the mass-only router. Cross-owner receiving requires a coordinator or a separate durable transfer protocol. Two unrelated owner commits would not establish atomic transfer, and marginal parcel error widths cannot be subtracted from a source-wide uncertainty bound.
